Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Japan Countdown, developed by Rich Code Studio, handles information.

Plain-English summary: Japan Countdown is designed to work offline and does not use your data for tracking or advertising.

Information Stored on Your Device

Trip dates, destinations, saved phrases, preferences, and checklist progress are stored locally on your device. Rich Code Studio does not receive this information.

Permissions

Japan Countdown does not require camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo-library access for its core features.

Tracking and Advertising

Japan Countdown does not track you across apps or websites and does not use your data for advertising.

Children's Privacy

Japan Countdown is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@richcodestudio.com.
